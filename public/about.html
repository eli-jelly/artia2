ABOUT THE APP

Here is some information that can help you understand the app better!

From Inspiration to Your Room

Found a room you love but are unsure how to recreate the same style in your own space? Our app is designed to bridge that gap by turning any inspiration image into clear, practical and personalised design ideas tailored specifically to your room. Whether it's a cosy bedroom, a modern living space, or a minimalist office, we help you understand how to translate what you see into something achievable in your own home.

See What Needs to Change

Upload images of your current room alongside your chosen inspiration, and our app will carefully compare the two. It highlights the key differences and provides thoughtful suggestions across colours, furniture, layout, lighting, materials and décor. Instead of vague advice, you get structured insights that show exactly what is working, what is missing, and what could be improved to bring your space closer to your vision.

Your Personal Design Guide

Rather than leaving you overwhelmed with ideas, we give you a clear, step-by-step personalised design guide. Each recommendation is easy to follow and prioritised so you know where to start. You can save your reports to your personal library, revisit past designs anytime, and track how your space evolves over time. This way, improving your room becomes a simple, guided and enjoyable process rather than a guessing game.
